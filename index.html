About us

A corner shop with a very serious roaster.

Brew & Bean began with one second-hand roaster and a rule: if we wouldn't drink it ourselves, it doesn't go on the counter. Seven years later, the roaster is still in the back and the rule hasn't changed.

We buy from farms we can name, bake with flour from a mill down the road, and learn the regulars' orders by heart.
